Product overview · honest status

A structured way to run outreach — without the busywork.

Outreach Pipelines is intended to help small teams track who they need to reach, what was said, and what happens next. No feature is described as live unless it is verified in the codebase — and today, no hosted product exists yet.

Intended workflow

From scattered messages to a clear pipeline.

Stage A

Capture

Add contacts and the reason for reaching out, with shared notes so context survives volunteer turnover.

Stage B

Organize

Place each outreach in a stage. Assign an owner and a next action with a due date.

Stage C

Follow through

Work from a “what needs attention” view: overdue follow-ups, unanswered messages, upcoming commitments.

Stage D

Learn

Review a simple activity history to see what moved forward and refine the next outreach round.

What it is

A lightweight organizer for outreach work: stages, ownership, reminders, and history — designed for teams of 1–20, not enterprise sales floors.

What it is not

No mass-email blasting, no scraped contact lists, no autonomous AI outreach, no hidden data collection. Bulk or unsolicited messaging would violate the project’s acceptable-use direction.

Why AI may help — later

AI where it reduces typing, never where it replaces judgment.

If pursued, AI would be limited to assistive, disclosed roles: drafting a first version of a message from the team’s own notes, summarizing a long thread for handoff, or suggesting a next step. Every output would require human review, and AI use would be opt-in per team.

No AI features exist today. The site makes no “AI-powered” claim about a current product.

Design constraints for any future AI

  • ✓Human approves before anything is sent
  • ✓Outputs labelled as AI-assisted where shown
  • ✓Works only on data the organization provided
  • ✓Can be switched off entirely
